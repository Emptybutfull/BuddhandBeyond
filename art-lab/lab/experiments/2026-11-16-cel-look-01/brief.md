# cel-look-01 (CEL_LOOK.md Step 1, the look test)

**Intent:** prove the clear-line cel look on one simple original scene before any story work.

**Scene:** a low-angle look across a plain at a small three-legged beacon (the man-made object; it carries the
accent), faceted rocks, a crystal cluster, far ridges, a big pale disc in the sky.

**Palettes:** `look/palettes/amber_day.json` (cream, sand, terracotta, plum; accent teal) and
`look/palettes/charcoal_night.json` (navy to slate blue; accent glowing amber).

**Good looks like:** every item in the CEL_LOOK.md look spec: thin even lines of 2 to 3 px in a dark tint (not
black), flat fills with exactly one hard shadow tone, shadows shifted in hue, 5 to 8 colours, the accent on one
object only, a gradient in the sky only, glow on the accent only.

**Tests:** both outline methods (Grease Pencil Line Art and inverted hull), and `tools/look_check.py`.

Stills only, 1920x1080. Not yet approved: after approval they become `look/approved_day.png` and
`look/approved_night.png`.
