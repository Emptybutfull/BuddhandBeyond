# sky-dance-01: design frames

**Intent:** find the look of "The Buddha dancing in the sky free as a bird strong as a mountain vast as the ocean"
in three key frames before anything is animated.

**Palette:** `look/palettes/azure_sky.json`. A blue world (pale sky to deep navy, cloud cream). One accent,
saffron `#ffb347`, on his robe and on the line of light his hand draws. Nothing else is warm.

**Good looks like:** the approved cel look (`look/approved_day.png`): thin even line, flat fill, one hard shadow
tone; the eye lands on him first; each frame says its one phrase without words (dancing, mountain, ocean).

**Tests:** the rule candidate "the clear-line cel look leads"; whether a figure drawn in code can stand in
that look next to Blender backgrounds; the line of light as the story thread.

Stills only, 1920x1080. Shot list: `story/shotlist.md`.
