# title-card-01 (E5, first test)

**Intent:** a title card that asks its question with one shape: a circle that looks whole comes apart
into five rings with nothing at the centre.

**Palette:** ground `#0a0c10`, type bone `#e9e2d2`, rings grey-blue `#7f8b9b`, accent amber `#ffb060`.

**Good looks like:** readable in one glance; the type is the brightest thing; three moves only (the rings
part, the title arrives, the accent leaves the centre and lands on the question mark) and then it holds;
nothing moves at a constant speed.

**Tests:** ART_LAB.md E5 principles (no linear easing, overlapping offsets, two or three moves, let it hold)
and the eye test. Typography is a guess: there is no STYLE.md yet (serif title, mono label).

The title text "Where is your self?" is a placeholder taken from an earlier video; swap in the current essay.

6 s, 1920x1080, 24 fps, Remotion.
