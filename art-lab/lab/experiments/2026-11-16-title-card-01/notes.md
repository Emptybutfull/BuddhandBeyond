# title-card-01: notes

**Made with:** Remotion 4.0.539 (`TitleCard.tsx`, composition `TitleCard01`, 144 frames at 24 fps), the design
kit's fonts (Instrument Serif for the title, JetBrains Mono for the label) and its `Grain` at 0.10.
`still.png` is frame 120 (5.0 s, the hold).

**Moves (three, overlapping, none linear):**
1. 0.55 to 3.4 s: one ring comes apart into five, each leaving 0.11 s after the last (ease in-out).
2. 1.5 to 2.8 s: the title arrives word by word (ease out).
3. 2.9 to 3.95 s: the amber dot at the centre fades as the amber question mark arrives.
Then it holds for two seconds; the rings drift by a pixel or two.

**Revisions on my own:** none after the first still.

**Eye test:** the type is the brightest thing (max 1.0 against a frame mean of 0.07). The accent covers 0.08%
of the frame in the hold: only the question mark.

**What a critical viewer notices first:** the title, then the loose knot of rings. It reads in one glance.

**Weak:**
- The typography is my guess. There is no STYLE.md, so serif title plus mono label is unconfirmed.
- The five rings are an idea you only get if you know the five aggregates; to anyone else it is a pleasant
  scribble.
- The label is very faint and small; on a phone it may vanish.
- Lots of empty frame. Calm, but it could feel under-designed next to the cel look.
- "Where is your self?" is a placeholder title.

**Next I would try:** the same card in the cel look (flat colour, ink line) to see which family the channel
wants; a version where the accent dot drifts down and becomes the dot of the question mark.
