# Characters

## The Buddha (original design for this piece)
- **Build:** adult, slender and upright, about seven heads tall; calm weight even in the air.
- **Head:** rounded crown bump (ushnisha) over short dark hair drawn as one flat navy shape; long earlobes;
  eyes closed in a soft downward arc; a slight smile; small dot between the brows.
- **Robe:** one saffron robe (`#ffb347`, shadow `#e07f2a`), over the left shoulder, right shoulder and arm bare,
  falling to the shins and trailing in the air. The robe is the film's accent colour; nothing else uses it.
- **Skin:** warm cream (`#f2d9bd`) with a lavender shadow (`#b79bb8`), so shadows shift toward the sky.
- **Distinctive item:** the line of light his right hand draws. Same saffron, with the only glow in the frame.
- **How he moves:** few poses, each held; a turn, an opening of the arms, a landing. Never busy. On twos.

Key poses: `rise` (drifting up, hands low), `turn` (one arm curved overhead), `wing` (arms wide, legs trailing),
`mountain` (wide stance, arms low and open, cloth hanging straight), `ocean` (arms flung wide, one knee lifted).

## The bird
- A white seabird, long narrow wings, drawn in cloud cream with the cloud shadow tone. No accent.
