# Recording yourself (for Steven — one page)

## Set-up (10 minutes, once)
- **Camera**: phone or camera on a tripod at eye level, horizontal, 4K or 1080p, **30 fps**
  (same as the video). Lock exposure/focus on your face if the phone allows.
- **Frame**: chest up, eyes about one third from the top, a hand's width of space above your head.
  Sit centred — the side panel and corner circle crop around your face.
- **Light**: face a window or put a soft lamp 45° to one side. Light on the face, not behind you.
- **Background**: your shrine corner works (statue, plant, candle, a little out of focus).
  For the cut-out halo shots, a plain wall that contrasts with your clothes makes the cut cleaner.
- **Clothes**: solid, darker colours. No fine stripes or logos.
- **Sound**: keep the Plaud clipped on your chest as the main mic. Start both, then **clap once on
  camera** — that lets the edit line them up to the frame.

## While you talk
Talk the way you always do. Just look into the lens for these moments (they become the on-camera
shots; the rest goes over art):
1. **The hook** — your strongest line, or a short personal moment, then the big question.
   ("I used to think… / What if…?")
2. **Each new idea** — say its first sentence to the lens.
3. **One thing for the viewer to try** — "Try this right now…" Pause where they'd do it.
4. **One story** — a Zen or sutra story you love. (This one plays over pictures.)
5. **The flip** — end an idea by turning it over ("We think it's X. It's actually Y.").
6. **The goodbye** — thank them plainly; ask for one thing (a comment, a share).

Pauses are fine and mistakes are fine — just start the sentence again; it gets cut.

## After
Drop the camera file and the Plaud recording in the project folder (or say which Plaud file).
That's it — Claude lines them up.
