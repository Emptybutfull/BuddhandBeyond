# Reference channels (watched Oct 2026 — don't re-watch, it costs credits)

Three channels Steven picked as the look to learn from. Notes are from vidIQ scene-by-scene watches
plus transcripts. "Borrow" = already built into format-blueprint.md / the motion kit.

## Scipio — "Do not Fear the Kali Yuga" (v9t_9wgEZeU, 20:54, 23k views, outlier 6.4×)
History-of-ideas cosmology essay, fully scripted, calm baritone. Channel hits: "A Theory about the
Last Time the World Ended" (217k), "Let's Build the Tower of Babel", "Every End of the World Explained".
- **Hook (0:00–1:15)**: cosmic-scale fact cascade (4 yugas, ratios, "this is the age we live in")
  over a rotating Yuga wheel → host on camera → first chapter card.
- **Chapters**: 14 timestamps, 1.5–3 min each. Card = ONE huge white word ("MARDUK", "GODS"),
  60–70% of width, smash-cut in over a darkened classical relief, sub-bass hit, then a beat of silence.
- **Host on camera ~15.6%**: 16 appearances, 7–21 s each (median ~13 s), ALL as a posterised/
  solarised duotone triptych (centre square + two mirrored copies) over a themed backdrop
  (Sistine ceiling, Brahma statue, storm clouds). Entered/exited with hard cuts or a small glitch.
  **Rule he follows: on camera only for chapter anchors, synthesis/opinion and meta asides. Never for
  exposition — history, myth and quotes always play over pictures.**
- **Pictures**: 30% retro science/educational film, 25% classical art, 20% animated diagrams,
  15% host, 10% text/maps. Shots 2–4 s. Slow Ken Burns + 2.5D cut-outs.
- **Diagrams**: rotating line-art dials (Yuga pie in 4:3:2:1, zodiac clock, precession orbits);
  segments flare as they are named; dial returns at the very end (bookend).
- **Texture**: 16mm dust/scratches, CRT lines, light leaks; magenta/purple/red cosmic palette.
- **Audio**: ambient drone + Eastern instruments, cuts locked to the beat, silence before big points.
- **Ending**: a Zen parable (Ryōkan and the thief — "I wish I could have given him this moon"),
  back to the opening wheel, logo sting, lowercase "check out more of my videos :)".
- **Title style**: reframe a feared idea — "Do not Fear the Kali Yuga".

## Broken Ruune — "Conspiracy Theorists Ask the WRONG Questions" (FLCHfyVexZk, 20:53, 609k, 11.5×)
First-person, conversational, partly unscripted ("this one was me pouring my heart out") — the
closest match to Steven's Plaud talks. Also watched "Buddhism Is Incoherent" (0Qorv5W1KGo, 135k).
- **Hook**: personal confession ("From a young age I've been curious…") → pivot question at ~0:30
  ("who developed this game?") → thesis stated plainly by ~1:00.
- **No chapter cards**: blocks of 2–4 min, turned by thought experiments ("say apple… now it's in the
  past — feel that vertigo?", "look at an anthill next time") and quiet music changes.
- **Permanent frame**: art plays in a ~4:3 window with a thin yellow outline, inside a looping
  starfield; mascot (cat on books) bottom-left, host slot bottom-right.
- **Host**: only in the first ~17 s, green-screen cut-out in the bottom-right corner, then gone.
- **Pictures**: 40% esoteric/classical/tarot, 35% retro sci-fi/anime/AI, 15% memes & TV, 5% text.
  For Buddhism: thangkas, Bhavacakra, visionary art, Wojak monk. Every image changes every 3–10 s
  on the clause; mostly hard cuts; stills almost static.
- **Glue**: one heavy texture (creased vinyl/paper + scanlines) over EVERYTHING, so memes and
  thangkas sit together without clashing.
- **Audio**: dry, forward voice; low ambient synth; **no whooshes or SFX at all**.
- **Ending**: sincere spoken CTA (Patreon, no sponsors), "LINK IN PINNED COMMENT" card.
- **Title style**: contrarian, one word in CAPS — "Ask the WRONG Questions", "Buddhism Is Incoherent".

## Hidden AmuraKa — "Timothy Leary: Acid King or Transhumanist Prophet?" (3xvIcbIV59Y, 47:31, 43k)
Long documentary essay, deep deliberate narration, never on camera. Channel hits run 20–80 min.
- **Cold open (0:00–1:08)**: rapid collage of the subject's own archival interview clips →
  branded title sequence with music crescendo → "So here's the pressing question…" at 1:28.
- **Sponsor at ~4:30**, bridged from the theme ("…another modern struggle over control"), then
  "Now, back to the man who…". Patreon cards as small corner watermarks mid-roll.
- **Chapters**: terminal-style cards phrased as claims/questions ("Reality is an Interface").
- **Signatures**: Rosetta-stone comparison tables (Sigil = executable code, Gnosis = hacked
  interface); flowchart posters for multi-stage models (8-circuit model, circuit by circuit);
  declassified-document look; every section ends by **inverting its own claim**
  ("He thought man would hack the machine; the machine hacked man").
- **Writing**: aphoristic triads ("The soul was information. Death was data loss. Resurrection was
  an engineering problem."), a micro-question every 2–3 min to reopen curiosity.
- **Pictures**: 40% archival TV/film, 25% custom diagrams, 15% memes, 10% AI art, 10% text/docs.
  Shots 2–4 s, diagrams 5–8 s with animated highlight boxes. CRT/VHS glitch everywhere.
- **Ending**: one closing aphorism, VHS "THE END" card.
- **Title style**: "Name: A or B?" / "The X: How Y Became Z".
