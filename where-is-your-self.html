    Key Teachings

    "Grasping towards pleasant sensations, having aversion towards unpleasant sensations, and misapprehending neutral sensations is what's causing us to suffer."

    Contact & feeling (vedana): when a sense meets its object, a feeling arises: pleasant, unpleasant or neutral. Suffering comes from how we grab at or push away those feelings.

    Relax and see clearly: relaxing body and mind gathers scattered energy (shamatha); directing that unified mind at what arises is clear seeing (vipassana).

    "When these sensations are seen as a passing show, like seeing leaves float by while standing on a bridge, the grasping tendency starts to relax."

    Emptiness (shunyata): objects, and the body itself, are made of many causes and conditions: mom, dad, food, the earth, memories. Look for the separate self in thoughts or sensations and you won't find it.

    "It is relationship in motion. It is through causes and conditions that appearances arise, like a rainbow."

    Interbeing: what grows out of seeing emptiness is not coldness but love, compassion and connection.
